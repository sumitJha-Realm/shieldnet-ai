RISK SCORE QUICK GUIDE
================================================

0-20:   GREEN - ALLOWED (Safe websites)
21-40:  YELLOW - LOW RISK (Newer/unverified)
41-60:  ORANGE - MEDIUM RISK (Questionable)
61-75:  RED - HIGH RISK (Likely malicious)
76-100: BLOCKED - CRITICAL (Definitely malicious)
